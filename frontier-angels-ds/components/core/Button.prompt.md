Branded button for all Frontier Angels CTAs and actions.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary">Invest Now</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" size="sm">Learn More</Button>
<Button variant="accent">Apply to Fund 5</Button>
<Button variant="ghost">View Portfolio</Button>
<Button variant="accent-outline" size="lg">Contact Us</Button>
```

**Variants:** `primary` (navy fill) | `secondary` (outlined) | `ghost` (text only) | `accent` (gold fill) | `accent-outline` | `danger`
**Sizes:** `xs` `sm` `md` `lg` `xl`
**Props:** `fullWidth`, `leftIcon`, `rightIcon`, `disabled`
