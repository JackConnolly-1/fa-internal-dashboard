Status badges and category tags throughout Frontier Angels UIs.

```jsx
<Badge variant="navy">Fund 5</Badge>
<Badge variant="gold">Featured</Badge>
<Badge variant="navy-subtle" dot>Active</Badge>
<Badge variant="gold-subtle">Seed Stage</Badge>
<Badge variant="success" dot>Funded</Badge>
<Badge variant="outline">FinTech</Badge>
```

**Variants:** `navy` | `navy-subtle` | `gold` | `gold-subtle` | `outline` | `success` | `warning` | `error` | `neutral`
**Props:** `size` (sm/md/lg), `dot` (boolean leading dot)
